LP Locker

Locks Uniswap v4 LP positions on Base in a contract with no owner. Nobody can remove the liquidity before the unlock date. Trading fees can be collected at any time, by anyone, and always go to the beneficiary.

Locking sends each position NFT to the lock with transferFrom. Wallets that support batched calls (EIP-5792, e.g. MetaMask smart accounts) do it in one transaction; others ask once per position. Source: git.borodutch.com/backmeupplz/locker.
